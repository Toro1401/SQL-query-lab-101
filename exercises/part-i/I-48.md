**Input:** `t1(sales_id, year)` (11 rows).

## Solution

```sql
WITH params(y) AS(VALUES(2021))
SELECT sales_id
FROM t1,params
WHERE year BETWEEN y-2 AND y
GROUP BY sales_id
HAVING count(DISTINCT year)=3;
```

**Result and verification:** result 1: 1 row. [[4004]]

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** A salesperson qualifies by making at least one sale in each of the three years. The reference year is fixed at 2021 to match the source independently of the current date.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** adding 2019 for salesperson 2002 leaves 2002 and 4004 qualified.
