**Input:** `t1(date, quantity_adjustment)` (5 rows).

## Question

What is the inventory balance after each dated quantity adjustment?

## Solution

```sql
SELECT date,quantity_adjustment,sum(quantity_adjustment) OVER(
ORDER BY date,rowid ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW) inventory
FROM t1
ORDER BY date,rowid;
```

## Results

| date | quantity_adjustment | inventory |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2018-07-01 | 100 | 100 |
| 2018-07-02 | 75 | 175 |
| 2018-07-03 | -150 | 25 |
| 2018-07-04 | 50 | 75 |
| 2018-07-05 | -100 | -25 |

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** the balance starts at zero and adds one adjustment at a time. The ROWS frame follows the date and uses rowid to order movements on the same day. An explicit MovementID would be a better tie-breaker in production.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** with a second adjustment of -10 after +75 on July 2, the balances are 100, 175, 165, 15, 65, and -35.

```sql
INSERT INTO t1 VALUES ('2018-07-02',-10);
```
