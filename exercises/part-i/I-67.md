**Input:** `t1(student, birthday)` (9 rows).

## Question

Which people share a full date of birth?

## Solution

```sql
SELECT birthday,group_concat(student,', '
ORDER BY student)
FROM t1
GROUP BY birthday
HAVING count(*)>1;
```

## Results

| birthday | group_concat(student,&#x27;, &#x27;<br>ORDER BY student) |
| --- | --- |
| 2015-04-15 | Jacob, Susan, Tim |
| 2015-05-23 | Angie, Mike |

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** the query groups people by their full date of birth, including the year. It returns shared dates with the names sorted alphabetically.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** adding a unique date of birth leaves the full result unchanged.

```sql
INSERT INTO t1 VALUES ('Another','2016-04-15');
```
