**Input:** `t1(account_id, balance)` (7 rows).

## Solution

```sql
SELECT account_id
FROM t1
GROUP BY account_id
HAVING max(balance)<=0 AND count(balance)=count(*);
SELECT DISTINCT a.account_id
FROM t1 a
WHERE NOT EXISTS(
SELECT 1
FROM t1 b
WHERE b.account_id=a.account_id AND (b.balance>0 OR b.balance IS NULL));
```

**Result and verification:** result 1: 1 row. [[2002]] Result 2: 1 row. [[2002]]

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** both formulations return accounts that never had a positive balance. Zero is allowed. A NULL balance excludes the account because its history is unknown.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** both formulations accept an account with a single balance of zero.
