## Question

How can you list the items in both shopping carts side by side, including items found in only one cart?

## Solution

```sql
SELECT a.item AS cart1,b.item AS cart2
FROM t1 a
FULL
JOIN t2 b ON a.item=b.item;
```

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** the full join keeps items from either cart, including items with no match. Duplicate items produce every matching pair.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** Only A appears once, with cart2 set to NULL.
