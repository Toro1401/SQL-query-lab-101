**Input:** `t1(team, year, score)` (8 rows).

## Question

What is each team's average score after removing the score furthest from its original mean?

## Solution

```sql
WITH a AS(
SELECT *,avg(1.0*score) OVER(PARTITION BY team) mean
FROM t1),b AS(
SELECT *,row_number() OVER(PARTITION BY team
ORDER BY abs(score-mean) DESC,year) rn
FROM a)
SELECT team,cast(avg(1.0*score) AS INTEGER)
FROM b
WHERE rn>1
GROUP BY team;
```

## Results

| team | cast(avg(1.0*score) AS INTEGER) |
| --- | --- |
| Bulldogs | 61 |
| Cougars | 53 |

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** for each team, the query removes the score furthest from its original average. A tie goes to the earliest year. The new average is truncated to match the source. A team with just one score has no remaining average.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** adding a team with only one observation leaves the full result unchanged.
