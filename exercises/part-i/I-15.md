## Solution

```sql
SELECT group_concat(syntax,' '
ORDER BY sequence)
FROM t1;
```

**Result and verification:** result 1: 1 row. [["SELECT Product, UnitPrice, EffectiveDate FROM Products WHERE UnitPrice > 100"]]

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** the query joins the fragments into one row in Sequence order. The sequence key must be unique.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** the new fragment appears at the end, separated by a space.
