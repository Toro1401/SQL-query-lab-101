## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** the SQLite adaptation registers SHA-256 and CRC32 in the runner in place of HASHBYTES and CHECKSUM. It hashes a canonical JSON array that distinguishes NULL from strings. CRC32 differs from SQL Server's CHECKSUM and does not guarantee uniqueness. A hash does not replace a UNIQUE constraint on the natural key.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** NULL is serialized unambiguously. The SHA-256 value is 64 hexadecimal characters long.
