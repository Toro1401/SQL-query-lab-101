**Input:** `t1(order_id, customer_id, amount)` (4 rows).

## Solution

```sql
SELECT *
FROM t1
WHERE customer_id IS NOT 1001 OR amount IS NOT 50;
```

**Result and verification:** result 1: 3 rows. [[1,1001,25],[3,2002,65],[4,3003,50]]

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** the query excludes the combination of customer 1001 and amount 50. The IS NOT conditions keep rows with NULL values that do not match that combination.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** A row with a NULL customer and amount 50 stays in the result.

```sql
INSERT INTO t1 VALUES (5,NULL,50);
```
