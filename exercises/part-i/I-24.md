**Input:** `t1(order_id, customer_id, order_date, amount, state)` (13 rows).

## Question

Which orders fall in positions 5 through 10 once they are sorted?

## Solution

```sql
SELECT *
FROM t1
ORDER BY order_id
LIMIT 6 OFFSET 4;
```

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** the page contains orders at positions 5 through 10 after sorting by the unique OrderID. Those positions can differ from the ID values.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** removing the first order shifts the page to IDs 6 through 11.

```sql
DELETE FROM t1 WHERE order_id=1;
```
