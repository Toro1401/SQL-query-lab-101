**Input:** `t1(vehicle_id, type, model, price)` (8 rows).

## Solution

```sql
SELECT a.model,b.model
FROM t1 a
CROSS
JOIN t1 b
WHERE a.type='Car' AND b.type='Boat' AND a.price>=b.price+200000;
```

**Result and verification:** result 1: 5 rows. [["Rolls-Royce Phantom","ATX 22-S"],["Rolls-Royce Phantom","Malibu"],["Rolls-Royce Phantom","Mastercraft"],["Lamborghini Spyder","ATX 22-S"],["Lamborghini Spyder","Mastercraft"]]

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** each car and boat pair must differ in price by at least 200000. The expected output treats the source wording “200000 more” as an inclusive threshold.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** the Lamborghini priced at 290000 matches the boat priced at 90000 because the difference is exactly 200000.

```sql
INSERT INTO t1 VALUES (9,'Boat','Exact',90000);
```
