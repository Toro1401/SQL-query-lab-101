## Question

Which states qualify when every customer must have an average of monthly order averages above 100?

## Solution

```sql
WITH monthly AS(
SELECT state,customer_id,substr(order_date,1,7) month,avg(1.0*amount) m
FROM t1
GROUP BY state,customer_id,substr(order_date,1,7)), customers AS(
SELECT state,customer_id,avg(m) m
FROM monthly
GROUP BY state,customer_id)
SELECT state
FROM customers
GROUP BY state
HAVING min(m)>100;
```

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** A state qualifies when every customer has an average of monthly averages above 100. Months without orders are excluded. This accounts for the expected TX result even though some months for customer 1001 average exactly 100.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** adding a TX customer with an average of zero excludes the state.

```sql
INSERT INTO t1 VALUES (14,9999,'2018-01-01',0,'TX');
```
