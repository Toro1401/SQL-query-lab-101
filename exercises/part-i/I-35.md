**Input:** `t1(invoice_id, sales_rep_id, amount, sales_type)` (8 rows).

## Solution

```sql
SELECT sales_rep_id
FROM t1
WHERE sales_type IN('Domestic','International')
GROUP BY sales_rep_id
HAVING count(DISTINCT sales_type)=1;
```

**Result and verification:** result 1: 4 rows. [[3003],[4004],[5005],[6006]]

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** the result has one row per representative who sold in exactly one category. Multiple sales in that category count as one category.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** adding another sale in the same category leaves the full result unchanged.

```sql
INSERT INTO t1 VALUES (9,3003,1,'Domestic');
```
