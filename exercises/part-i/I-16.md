**Input:** `t1(player_a, player_b, score)` (3 rows).

## Question

How can you combine scores for the same pair when the two members can appear in either order?

## Solution

```sql
SELECT min(player_a,player_b),max(player_a,player_b),sum(score)
FROM t1
GROUP BY min(player_a,player_b),max(player_a,player_b);
```

## Results

| min(player_a,player_b) | max(player_a,player_b) | sum(score) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1001 | 2002 | 150 |
| 3003 | 4004 | 140 |

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** the query puts the two endpoints in a canonical order, then sums the scores for each unordered pair.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** adding the reversed pair increases the total by 10 without creating a new pair.
