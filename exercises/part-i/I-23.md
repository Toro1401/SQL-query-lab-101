**Input:** `t1(player_id, score)` (5 rows).

## Solution

```sql
SELECT ntile(2) OVER(
ORDER BY score DESC,player_id),player_id,score
FROM t1;
```

**Result and verification:** result 1: 5 rows. [[1,2002,9432],[1,5005,6832],[1,3003,6548],[2,1001,2343],[2,4004,1054]]

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** each player goes into one of two balanced groups. If the total is odd, the first half gets the extra player. The ID breaks score ties.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** six players split three per half. Players with equal scores are ordered by ID.
