**Input:** `t1(days, seed)` (1 row).

## Question

What fraction of days is rainy under the given Markov transition probabilities?

## Solution

```sql
WITH RECURSIVE r(day,rain) AS(VALUES(1,0)
UNION ALL
SELECT day+1,CASE WHEN rnd((
SELECT seed
FROM t1)+day)%100<CASE rain WHEN 1 THEN 70 ELSE 50 END THEN 1 ELSE 0 END
FROM r
WHERE day<(
SELECT days
FROM t1))
SELECT count(*),sum(rain),avg(rain)
FROM r;
```

## Results

| count(*) | sum(rain) | avg(rain) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100000 | 62680 | 0.6268 |

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** one row summarizes a 100,000-day chain with the total days, rainy days, and the rainy fraction. The first day is sunny. The chance of rain the next day is 0.5 after sun and 0.7 after rain. The theoretical stationary fraction is 5/8, or 0.625.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** A one-day chain starts sunny and has zero rainy days.

```sql
UPDATE t1 SET days=1;
```
