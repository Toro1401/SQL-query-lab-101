## Solution

```sql
WITH RECURSIVE people(x) AS(
SELECT 1
UNION ALL
SELECT x+1
FROM people
WHERE x<10),r(k,mask,seats,prev) AS(
SELECT 0,0,'',0
UNION ALL
SELECT k+1,mask|(1<<x),seats||CASE WHEN k=0 THEN '' ELSE ',' END||x,x
FROM r
CROSS
JOIN people
WHERE (mask&(1<<x))=0 AND (k>=4 OR x<=(
SELECT adults
FROM t1)) AND (k NOT IN(6,8,9) OR x>prev))
SELECT seats
FROM r
WHERE k=10;
```

## Explanation

**Method and assumptions:** positions 1 through 4 are the drivers of the motorcycle, sidecar, golf cart, and car. Position 5 is the sidecar passenger, 6 and 7 are golf cart passengers, and 8 through 10 are car passengers. People 1 through 5 are adults and 6 through 10 are children. Ordering passengers within each vehicle avoids duplicate arrangements. There are 120 driver choices and 60 passenger assignments per choice, for 7,200 arrangements.

**Edge cases and trade-offs:** three adults cannot drive four vehicles, so no arrangements qualify.

UPDATE t1 SET adults=3;
